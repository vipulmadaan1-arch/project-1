Tools/Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

The short version

The calculators run entirely in your browser. The figures you enter, such as your salary or a property price, are never sent to a server, and there is no account to create. If the site shows ads, Google may use cookies to choose them, as explained below.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings. Your light or dark theme, the tools you pin and your world clock cities are saved in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser data removes them.
  • Offline copy. Your browser keeps a copy of the site's files so the tools work without an internet connection. It holds no personal information.
  • Results links. When you choose “Copy link”, the calculator's inputs are added to the link so the result can be shared. Anyone you send that link to can see those figures. When a results link is opened, the figures fill in the calculator and are then removed from the page address.

What we don't collect

There are no analytics or tracking scripts of our own, and no forms that send your details anywhere. Fonts and icons are served from this site, not from a third party.

Advertising

To keep the tools free, the site may show ads provided by Google AdSense. When ads are shown:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings. Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

Ad scripts can see the address of the page you're viewing. Calculator inputs are kept out of the page address while you use a tool, and nothing you type is sent to us or to advertisers by the calculators themselves.

Other services

  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Buy Me a Coffee. If you choose to support the site, the payment is handled on Buy Me a Coffee's website under its own privacy policy. We don't see your card details.
  • Links to official sites. Links to the ATO, Revenue NSW and other sites take you to services with their own privacy policies.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.
