How it works

Gross yield = annual rent ÷ purchase price × 100. It's the quick comparison figure used in listings.

Net yield = (annual rent after vacancy − annual costs) ÷ purchase price × 100. It's a better guide to what the property actually earns before mortgage interest and tax.

Example: $520/week on a $550,000 property is $27,040 a year — a 4.92% gross yield.

Frequently asked questions

What costs should I include?

Council and water rates, landlord insurance, strata levies, property management fees, repairs and maintenance, and land tax if it applies.

Does this include mortgage repayments?

No. Yield measures the property's return on its price. Use the mortgage calculator for repayments and consider your cash flow separately.

What's a good rental yield?

It depends on the location, property type and growth prospects. Higher-yield areas often have lower capital growth, and vice versa.
