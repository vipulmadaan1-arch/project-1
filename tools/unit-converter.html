How it works

Convert between metric and imperial units for length, mass, volume, area, speed and temperature. Australia uses metric, but you'll still meet feet, pounds and Fahrenheit in recipes, specs and overseas listings.

Australian real estate sometimes quotes house size in squares: one square is 100 square feet, or about 9.29 m².

Frequently asked questions

Is a cup the same everywhere?

No. This converter uses the Australian metric cup of 250 mL. A US cup is about 237 mL.

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

°F = °C × 9/5 + 32. So 25 °C is 77 °F.
