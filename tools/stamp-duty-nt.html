How NT stamp duty is calculated (2026-27)

Rates checked 15 Oct 2026 against the Territory Revenue Office and NT Treasury Budget Paper 2.

Northern Territory stamp duty on a property transfer is charged on the dutiable value, the higher of the price and the unencumbered value. Up to $525,000 it's worked out with a formula from the Territory Revenue Office; above that it's a flat percentage of the whole value. The same rates apply to homes and investments.

Dutiable valueStamp duty
Up to $525,000(0.06571441 × V²) + 15V, where V is the value ÷ 1,000
$525,001 – under $3 million4.95% of the value
$3 million – under $5 million5.75% of the value
$5 million and over5.95% of the value

Worked example: a $500,000 home: V = 500, so (0.06571441 × 250,000) + (15 × 500) = 16,428.60 + 7,500 = $23,929. A $750,000 home is 4.95% × $750,000 = $37,125.

House and land package exemption: buy a new detached home and its land from a building contractor in one contract, signed between 1 July 2022 and 30 June 2027, and you pay no stamp duty. It isn't means tested, there's no price cap and you don't need to be a first home buyer, but at least one buyer must live in it for 6 continuous months, starting within 12 months of completion. The NT has no other first home buyer duty concession and no foreign buyer surcharge.

Confirm with the Territory Revenue Office's exemption page and its stamp duty calculator. The rates above $3 million come from NT Treasury's 2024-25 Budget Paper 2. Buying in another state? Compare them in the all-states stamp duty calculator.

NT stamp duty FAQs

How much is stamp duty on a $500,000 home in the NT?

$23,929, from the Territory Revenue Office formula (0.06571441 × V²) + 15V with V = 500. It's the same for a first home buyer, unless it's a house and land package, which pays $0.

Do first home buyers pay stamp duty in the NT?

Yes, on an established home: $23,929 on $500,000 and $37,125 on $750,000. The only home buyer exemption is for a house and land package bought from a builder in one contract, signed by 30 June 2027, which any buyer who lives in it can use.

What is the NT house and land package exemption?

No stamp duty on a new detached home and its land bought from a building contractor in a single contract, signed between 1 July 2022 and 30 June 2027. No price cap or means test; one buyer must live in it for 6 months, starting within 12 months of completion.

How much is stamp duty on a $750,000 home in Darwin?

$37,125: above $525,000 the NT charges 4.95% of the whole value, rising to 5.75% from $3 million and 5.95% from $5 million.

Is there a foreign buyer surcharge in the NT?

No. The Northern Territory doesn't charge foreign buyers extra stamp duty, so a $750,000 purchase costs $37,125 either way.
