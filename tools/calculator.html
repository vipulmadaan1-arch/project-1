    How it works

    A simple, fast calculator that follows the normal order of operations — brackets first, then × and ÷, then + and −. Type directly on your keyboard: use * for ×, / for ÷, Enter for equals, Backspace to delete and Esc to clear.

    The % key divides by 100, so 50% × 200 = 100.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is anything I type saved or sent anywhere?

    No. Everything runs in your browser.

    Why does 0.1 + 0.2 show 0.3?

    Results are rounded to 12 decimal places to hide floating-point errors common in computer arithmetic.
