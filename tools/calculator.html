    How it works

    A simple, fast calculator that follows the normal order of operations — brackets first, then × and ÷, then + and −. Type directly on your keyboard: use * for ×, / for ÷, Enter for equals, Backspace to delete and Esc to clear.

    The % key divides by 100, so 50% × 200 = 100. A number next to a bracket multiplies, so 2(3 + 4) = 14. Press a different operator to replace the one you just pressed.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is anything I type saved or sent anywhere?

    No. Everything runs in your browser.

    Why does 0.1 + 0.2 show 0.3?

    Results are rounded to 15 significant digits to hide the tiny floating-point errors common in computer arithmetic. Very large and very small results are shown in scientific notation, so 1 ÷ 100,000,000,000 shows as 1e-11 rather than 0.
