HECS/HELP Debt Payoff: how it works

Rates checked 15 Oct 2026 against the ATO's study loan repayment rates.

Each year the ATO works out a compulsory repayment from your repayment income. From 2025-26 it's a marginal system: you only repay on income above the threshold.

Repayment income (2026-27)Compulsory repayment
$0 – $69,528Nil
$69,529 – $129,71715c for each $1 over $69,528
$129,718 – $186,050$9,028 plus 17c for each $1 over $129,717
$186,051 and over10% of total repayment income

On 1 June each year the ATO adds indexation to debt that's more than 11 months old, at the lower of CPI and wage growth (2.8% on 1 June 2026). The calculator repeats this year by year using your pay rise. It treats voluntary repayments as made before 1 June and compulsory repayments as coming off after each tax return. Future indexation isn't known, so it uses the rate you enter. Repayment thresholds are held at 2026-27 values; they normally rise each year, so the real payoff may take a little longer.

HECS/HELP Debt Payoff FAQs

How is HECS/HELP indexation worked out?

On 1 June each year the ATO indexes the part of your debt that's more than 11 months old, at the lower of the Consumer Price Index and the Wage Price Index. It was 2.8% on 1 June 2026, 3.2% in 2025 and 4% in 2024.

What happened with the 20% HECS cut?

The Government cut study loan debts by 20%, based on your balance at 1 June 2025, before that year's indexation. The ATO applied it in late 2025. Your balance in myGov already includes it, so enter that figure.

Is it worth making voluntary HECS repayments?

It saves indexation and can shorten the time you're repaying, which the calculator shows. There's no interest beyond indexation, so paying off higher-interest debt or building an offset balance often comes first. To beat a 1 June indexation, the ATO needs your payment in time; electronic payments can take up to 4 business days to arrive, so in 2026 that meant paying by Monday 25 May.

When do I have to start repaying HECS?

When your repayment income is above $69,528 in 2026-27. Below that there's no compulsory repayment, but indexation still applies each year.

Does my HECS debt get written off?

Not because of time or low income. HELP debts stay until repaid, apart from limited cases such as death or a special-circumstances cancellation.
