How SA stamp duty is calculated (2026-27)

Rates checked 15 Oct 2026 against RevenueSA.

South Australian stamp duty on a property transfer is charged on the dutiable value (the higher of the price and the market value) for every $100 or part of $100, and RevenueSA quotes it to the cent. The same scale applies whether you'll live in the home or rent it out: South Australia has no owner-occupier discount.

Dutiable valueStamp duty
$0 – $12,000$1.00 per $100
$12,001 – $30,000$120 + $2.00 per $100 over $12,000
$30,001 – $50,000$480 + $3.00 per $100 over $30,000
$50,001 – $100,000$1,080 + $3.50 per $100 over $50,000
$100,001 – $200,000$2,830 + $4.00 per $100 over $100,000
$200,001 – $250,000$6,830 + $4.25 per $100 over $200,000
$250,001 – $300,000$8,955 + $4.75 per $100 over $250,000
$300,001 – $500,000$11,330 + $5.00 per $100 over $300,000
Over $500,000$21,330 + $5.50 per $100 over $500,000

Worked example: a $600,000 home: $21,330 + $5.50 × 1,000 (the $100s over $500,000) = $26,830. A foreign buyer adds the 7% surcharge, $42,000, for $68,830.

First home buyers pay no stamp duty on a new home, an off-the-plan apartment or vacant land to build a home on, with no price cap for contracts signed on or after 6 June 2024. There's no relief on an established home. At least one buyer must be an Australian citizen or permanent resident, and you must live in the home for at least 6 continuous months, starting within 12 months of settlement (or of the home being ready, for land).

SA stamp duty FAQs

How much is stamp duty on a $600,000 home in South Australia?

$26,830: $21,330 plus $5.50 per $100 over $500,000. It's the same whether you live in it or rent it out. A first home buyer pays $0 if it's a new home, and a foreign buyer pays $68,830 including the 7% surcharge.

Do first home buyers pay stamp duty in SA?

$0 on a new home, an off-the-plan apartment or vacant land to build on, at any price, for contracts from 6 June 2024. On an established home, first home buyers pay full duty: $26,830 on $600,000.

Is there a price cap on SA first home buyer relief?

No, not for contracts signed on or after 6 June 2024. Between 15 June 2023 and 5 June 2024 new homes had to be worth under $700,000 and vacant land under $450,000.

How much is stamp duty on $500,000 and $750,000 in SA?

$21,330 on $500,000 and $35,080 on $750,000. Above $500,000 each extra $100 costs $5.50.

How much is the foreign buyer surcharge in SA?

7% of the dutiable value on top of normal duty: $42,000 on a $600,000 home. A foreign buyer on their own can't get first home buyer relief.
