How ACT stamp duty is calculated (2026-27)

Rates checked 22 Oct 2026 against the ACT Revenue Office.

ACT conveyance duty (stamp duty) is charged on the dutiable value, the higher of the price and the market value, for every $100 or part of $100. There are two scales: a lower one for owner-occupiers (a home you'll live in) and one for everyone else. Above $1,455,000 both are a flat 4.54% of the whole value. These are the ACT Revenue Office rates for 2026-27.

Worked example: a $750,000 home you'll live in: $8,408 + $4.32 × 2,500 (the $100s over $500,000) = $19,208. As an investment it's $11,400 + $4.32 × 2,500 = $22,200.

Home Buyer Concession Scheme: from 1 July 2026 the property price cap and the income threshold are gone, so eligible buyers pay no duty at any price. No buyer (or their domestic partner) can have owned property anywhere in the last five years, and at least one buyer must live in the home for at least 12 months, starting within a year of settlement. You don't have to be a first home buyer. The ACT charges no foreign buyer duty surcharge.

ACT stamp duty FAQs

How much is stamp duty on a $750,000 home in Canberra?

$19,208 if you'll live in it: $8,408 plus $4.32 per $100 over $500,000. As an investment it's $22,200. Under the Home Buyer Concession Scheme it's $0.

Who pays no stamp duty under the ACT Home Buyer Concession Scheme?

Buyers where no one, including domestic partners, has owned property anywhere in the last five years, and one of them lives in the home for at least 12 months. From 1 July 2026 there's no price cap and no income test, so a $750,000 home saves $19,208.

How much is stamp duty on a $600,000 home in the ACT?

$12,728 for an owner-occupier and $15,720 for an investor. Both scales charge $4.32 per $100 between $500,000 and $750,000; the owner-occupier scale is $2,992 cheaper at this price.

Is there a foreign buyer surcharge on ACT stamp duty?

No. The ACT charges no extra conveyance duty for foreign buyers, so a foreign buyer of a $750,000 investment pays the same $22,200.

What is the ACT stamp duty on a $1 million home?

$33,958 for an owner-occupier and $36,950 for an investor. Above $1,455,000 both pay a flat 4.54% of the whole value.
