How it works

  • X% of Y = X ÷ 100 × Y. 15% of 200 = 30.
  • X is what % of Y = X ÷ Y × 100. 30 is 25% of 120.
  • Percentage change = (new − old) ÷ old × 100. From 80 to 100 is +25%.

Note that a 25% increase followed by a 25% decrease doesn't get you back to where you started: 80 → 100 → 75.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add a percentage to a number?

Multiply by (1 + percent ÷ 100). To add 15% to 200: 200 × 1.15 = 230.

What's the difference between percent and percentage points?

If a rate goes from 5% to 6%, it rose by 1 percentage point, which is a 20% increase.
