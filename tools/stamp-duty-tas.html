How Tasmanian stamp duty is calculated (2026-27)

Rates checked 15 Oct 2026 against State Revenue Office Tasmania.

Tasmanian property transfer duty is charged on the dutiable value, the greater of the price and the unencumbered (market) value, for every $100 or part of $100. The same scale applies to homes and investments: Tasmania has no owner-occupier rate. These rates have applied to transfers since 21 October 2013.

Dutiable valueDuty
$0 – $3,000$50
$3,001 – $25,000$50 + $1.75 per $100 over $3,000
$25,001 – $75,000$435 + $2.25 per $100 over $25,000
$75,001 – $200,000$1,560 + $3.50 per $100 over $75,000
$200,001 – $375,000$5,935 + $4.00 per $100 over $200,000
$375,001 – $725,000$12,935 + $4.25 per $100 over $375,000
Over $725,000$27,810 + $4.50 per $100 over $725,000

Worked example: an $800,000 home: $27,810 + $4.50 × 750 (the $100s over $725,000) = $31,185. A foreign buyer adds the 8% surcharge, $64,000, for $95,185.

First home buyers: the exemption for established homes up to $750,000 is not available for transfers settling after 30 June 2026, so a first home buyer now pays the same duty as anyone else, for example $22,498 on a $600,000 home. The First Home Owner Grant is still paid for new homes. Pensioners downsizing to a new home can get a 50% concession.

Confirm rates and eligibility with State Revenue Office Tasmania and its concessions and exemptions. Buying in another state? Compare them in the all-states stamp duty calculator.

Tasmania stamp duty FAQs

How much is stamp duty on a $600,000 home in Tasmania?

$22,498: $12,935 plus $4.25 per $100 over $375,000. It's the same for a home, an investment or a first home buyer settling after 30 June 2026. A foreign buyer pays $70,498 including the 8% surcharge.

Do first home buyers still get a stamp duty exemption in Tasmania?

Not on transfers settling after 30 June 2026. The exemption for established homes up to $750,000 ran from 18 February 2024 to 30 June 2026, so a $600,000 first home now costs $22,498 in duty. Check grants for new homes with State Revenue Office Tasmania.

How much is stamp duty on $500,000 and $800,000 in Tasmania?

$18,248 on $500,000 and $31,185 on $800,000. Above $725,000 each extra $100 costs $4.50.

How much is the foreign investor duty surcharge in Tasmania?

8% of the dutiable value on top of normal duty: $48,000 on a $600,000 home.

Is duty charged on the price or the valuation?

On whichever is greater: the price you pay or the unencumbered value, which is the Valuer-General's valuation multiplied by your council area's adjustment factor.
