How it works

Checked 15 Oct 2026. Uses the same approach as Moneysmart's compound interest calculator, so you can cross-check a result there.

Compound interest means you earn interest on your interest. The balance grows by A = P(1 + r/n)nt, where P is the starting amount, r the annual rate, n the compounding periods per year and t the years. Regular deposits are added each period.

The year-by-year table shows how much of your balance comes from deposits and how much from interest. Over long periods, interest usually overtakes deposits.

Frequently asked questions

Does compounding frequency matter much?

A little. Daily compounding earns slightly more than monthly, and monthly slightly more than annually, at the same rate. Time and rate matter far more.

Does this include tax?

No. Interest earned in a savings account is taxable income in Australia. Results also ignore fees and rate changes.

Can I use this for investments?

Yes, as a rough guide with an expected average return, but investment returns vary year to year and aren't guaranteed.
