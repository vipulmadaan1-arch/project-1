How it works

Australia's GST rate is 10%.

  • Add GST: price × 1.1. $100 + GST = $110.
  • Remove GST: price ÷ 1.1. $110 includes $10 GST.
  • GST in a GST-inclusive price: price ÷ 11. For $220, GST is $20.

A common mistake is taking 10% off a GST-inclusive price — $110 − 10% = $99, not $100.

Frequently asked questions

Why divide by 11?

Because a GST-inclusive price is 110% of the base price, GST is 10/110 = 1/11 of the total.

Do I need to charge GST?

Generally, businesses must register for GST when their GST turnover is $75,000 or more. Check the ATO for your situation.
